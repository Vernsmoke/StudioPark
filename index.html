See what open tools can make.

Spring is an open animated film created by Blender Animation Studio. It demonstrates how story, sound, design, and 3D production come together in one multimedia experience.

Read about the film ↗ (opens in a new tab)
Video description and accessibility note

The film follows a shepherd girl and her dog as they face ancient spirits and help continue the cycle of spring. The video is dialogue-free and includes music and environmental sound. YouTube playback controls and captions are available when provided by the host.
